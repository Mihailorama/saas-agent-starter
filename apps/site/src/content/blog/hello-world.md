Workspace is a home for your team's projects. Each organization gets its own space, every change
is logged, and AI drafts summaries that a person confirms before anyone relies on them.

## What it does today

- Projects per organization, with roles for owners, admins and members.
- AI summaries you can confirm or correct, with every model call recorded.
- An audit trail you can export.
