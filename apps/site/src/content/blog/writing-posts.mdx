Posts live in `apps/site/src/content/blog/` as `.md` or `.mdx` files. The file name is the URL:
`writing-posts.mdx` becomes `/blog/writing-posts/`.

## Front matter

`title` (up to 60 characters), `description` (50 to 160), `pubDate`, `author`; optional
`updatedDate`, `draft: true` (only shown by `pnpm --filter site dev`) and `faq` (question/answer
pairs, rendered with FAQPage markup). The build fails when a field breaks these limits.

## Media

Images and video are not stored in git: host them (Drive, a CDN, your object storage) and link
them by absolute URL.
